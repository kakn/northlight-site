Northlight Studio

A private publishing tool

Northlight Studio is a personal tool its owner uses to publish videos the owner made to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. The owner reviews and approves each video before it is sent.

It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it. It asks each platform only for permission to upload videos to the owner's own account and to read that account's basic profile.

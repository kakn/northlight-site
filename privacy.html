Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Northlight Studio is a private tool its owner uses for publishing the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts. It has no users other than the owner.

What it receives

When the owner signs in through a platform (Google for YouTube, TikTok, Meta for Instagram and Facebook), Northlight Studio receives an access token for the owner's account and that account's basic profile (its id, name and avatar), and the answers the platform gives to its uploads.

How it is used

Only to upload the owner's approved videos to the owner's account and to show whether an upload worked. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Where it is kept

On the owner's own computer, never on a server. Tokens are kept in a local file only the owner can read, and can be removed at any time by deleting that file, or by removing Northlight Studio's access in the platform's settings.

YouTube

Northlight Studio uses the YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos to the owner's YouTube channel. Using it with YouTube means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Its access to a Google account can be removed at any time at Google's security settings.

Other people's data

Through the platforms' sign-ins and APIs, Northlight Studio receives nothing about other users.
